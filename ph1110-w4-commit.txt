feat(aegis): add real per-period and per-day trend aggregations

PH11.10 wave 4. Two required analytics could not be satisfied honestly from the
existing aggregates, so this adds the smallest aggregations that can.

Academic trend. `StudentMetricsView` reports `latest_period_average` computed
from its own overall average — the same figure twice, not two periods — so it
cannot form a series. `/analytics/subjects/trends/` aggregates the same grades
but keys its periods by name, giving "Second Term" before "First Term". Four new
endpoints return genuine per-period averages carrying `GradingPeriod.order`:
per-student and school-wide, the latter staff-or-admin.

Attendance trend. Neither `student_attendance_summary` (whole-period totals) nor
`/attendance/summary/` (per-section counts, no date axis) can plot attendance
over time. Two new endpoints aggregate real AttendanceRecord rows per day:
per-student, and school-wide for administrators only.

Authorization is shared, not copied. `_StudentScopedAnalyticsView` lifts the
ladder `StudentMetricsView` already enforced — own record / linked child /
teaching scope / guidance and admin — so the trends cannot drift from the
metrics route they sit beside. Tests pin every rung, including that a caller
refused by one route is refused by the other.

Two honesty rules the aggregations share. A period whose grades are all
unscored is reported with average None rather than zero, because zero asserts a
failing score the database does not contain. A day with no attendance record is
omitted rather than emitted as three zeroes, because nothing recorded is not the
same as everyone absent.

Also required: the PH11.10 charts for the risk and role distributions and the
new academic and attendance trends, and prettier formatting across the phase's
files.

Bundle: 1,328.91 kB raw / 371.50 kB gzip, against the ~500 kB advisory that
already existed. Recharts is the cost and the charts are the deliverable, so it
stands; code-splitting is a separate phase and was not bolted on here.
